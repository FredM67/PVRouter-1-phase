Detailed Description

The diverted energy measured on CT2: Wh total, anti-creep limit, reset after inactivity.

Robin's diverted energy of Mk2_fasterControl_Full (processLatestContribution()): once per mains cycle, the energy measured on CT2 is added to an accumulator if it reaches the anti-creep limit, and whole Wh are carried into a 16-bit total. After a long period without diversion (DISPLAY_SHUTDOWN_IN_HOURS), the total is reset, for the next day's data.

What differs from Full:

  • the cycles during which a local load or relay is forced (override pin, scheduled boost) are not counted at all: CT2 measures every local output, it cannot tell the forced load from the others (Full skips CT2 while its single load is forced);
  • "active" (Robin's EDD_isActive) means some energy counted since the last reset; the energy is counted from the first cycle, there is no gate;
  • the reset counts the cycles without diversion: without any local load or relay ON, or with one forced. Full counts on the diversion state of its first load, which an override does not change: a boost at night must not keep the day's total alive.

The energy unit (IEU) is the caller's: the router passes the cycle's average power in W x 2^Energy::FRACTION_BITS, the unit of its energy bucket (energy_bucket.h).

Pure C++, no Arduino: the native tests include it.
