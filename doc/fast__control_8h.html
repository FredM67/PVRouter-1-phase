Detailed Description

Faster control: the load decision on the predicted energy, at mid-cycle.

Robin Emley's fasterControl. At the first sample set of the negative half cycle, the average power of the first half is known: the energy at the end of the cycle is predicted from it. A few sample sets later (DECISION_SAMPLE), the loads are decided on that prediction. The zero-crossing triac drivers latch the new states at the next positive zero crossing: the loads change at the start of the next cycle, half a cycle sooner than with a decision taken after that crossing, on the measured bucket.

The ISR predicts through the per-sample calibration table, without a division (wholeCycleIndexOf()); the division form is kept as the reference of the tests.

Note
This header has no Arduino dependency, so it can be exercised by the native test suite.
