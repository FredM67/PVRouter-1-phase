Detailed Description

Native tests for sample_sets.h: the counter of the sample sets of a datalog period.

The counter is 16 bits while the largest count of a datalog period fits in it, 32 bits above: the default 5 s period keeps its 16-bit counter, periods up to 40 s are safe. Largest count = mains cycles of the period x N_MAX, the most sample sets in a cycle: 68 at 50 Hz (64 + 4), 57 at 60 Hz (53 + 4), with 3 conversions of 104 us per set.
