A compile-time number of hours, as MINUTES().

Usage: HOURS(2). Too many hours fail to compile instead of wrapping around: the product overflows the 16-bit int of the AVR (from HOURS(547) on), and is a narrowing conversion to uint16_t on a native build (beyond 65535 minutes).
