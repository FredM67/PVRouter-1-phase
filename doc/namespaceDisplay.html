Parameters

divertedCta CT2 is fitted (DIVERTED_CT_PRESENT)

Writes the temperature temperature_x100, rounded to 0.1 °C.

Parameters
lettersSEG: 'C' and '-' can be shown; SEG_HW: they cannot (the 74HC4543 shows 0 to 9 and a blank only)
