Detailed Description

Native tests for diverted_energy.h: the diverted energy measured on CT2.

One call of addCycle() per mains cycle, with the cycle's energy in integer energy units (IEU), whether a local load or relay is ON, and whether one is forced. The energy above the anti-creep limit is summed, whole Wh carried into a 16-bit total; a forced cycle is not counted; after SHUTDOWN_CYCLES cycles without diversion, everything is reset, as Robin's display shutdown. No config.h, no stubs.
