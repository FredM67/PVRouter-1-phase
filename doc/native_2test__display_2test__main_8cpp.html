Detailed Description

Native tests for display_core.h: the pins, port images and content of the display.

The pins are those of Mk2_fasterControl_Full. The port images are checked against the ATmega328P ports (D0-D7 on PORTD, D8-D13 on PORTB, D14-D19 on PORTC). No config.h, no stubs.
