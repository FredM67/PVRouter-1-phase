7-segment display functions

From Mk2_fasterControl_Full/utils_display.h. The display is multiplexed from the ADC interrupt, as in Full: one digit after another, every MAX_DISPLAY_TIME_COUNT sample sets (10 x 312 us, so each digit for about 3 ms and the whole display 80 times a second). Unlike Full, the interrupt does not look up the characters nor write the pins one at a time: the loop turns the 4 characters into the port images of the 4 digits (display_core.h), and the interrupt writes one image with a few masked port writes.

The ports are written with read-modify-write in the interrupt, which nothing interrupts. The loop never writes these ports that way (setPinState() and togglePin() go through PINx, digitalWrite() disables the interrupts), so no change is lost either way.

◆ refresh7SegDisplay()

void refresh7SegDisplay ( )
inline

Multiplexes the 7-segment display: the next digit, every MAX_DISPLAY_TIME_COUNT calls.

Called by the ADC interrupt once per sample set (with the CT1 sample), as in Full. Most calls only count. Every MAX_DISPLAY_TIME_COUNT-th call:

  1. nothing shows: every digit off (SEG), or the 74HC4543 off and the DP off (SEG_HW);
  2. the image of the next digit is written to ports B, C, then D. With SEG_HW, the enable line is on port D: the chip comes on once the BCD and digit lines are set. With SEG, the digit line comes on with its port, a few cycles before the segments of the ports written after it: about 0.2 us in 3 ms, nothing visible.
