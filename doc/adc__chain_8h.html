Detailed Description

The three ADC channels of each board, and the ADMUX pipeline of the free-running ADC.

The ADC converts three channels in a fixed chain, V -> I_GRID -> I_DIVERTED, each about every 312 us (3 conversions of 13 ADC clocks at F_CPU / 128).

The ADC runs free: when the interrupt of a finished conversion runs, the next conversion has already started with the previous ADMUX. The ADMUX written now applies to the conversion after next, so after the conversion of a channel the interrupt writes the channel two steps ahead in the chain.

Pure C++, no Arduino: the native tests include it. The ADMUX bit numbers are those of the ATmega328P (processing.cpp checks them against avr/io.h).
